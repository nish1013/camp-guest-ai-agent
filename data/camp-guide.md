# Camp guide

Each section starts with a `## Title {#section-id}` heading. Answers cite the section id, so keep
ids stable when the text changes. The `emergency` and `reception` sections are read once at
start-up; restart the app after changing them.

## Emergency {#emergency}

If anyone is in danger, call 999 now and leave the cabin. Then call reception on 01632 960123.
Reception has been alerted.

## Reception {#reception}

Call reception on 01632 960123. The desk is staffed 08:00-22:00.

## Pool hours {#pool-hours}

The pool is open 08:00-20:00 every day. Children under 12 must be with an adult.

## Check-out {#check-out}

Check-out is by 10:00 on the day you leave. Leave the key in the cabin.

## Restaurant {#restaurant}

The restaurant serves breakfast 07:30-10:00 and dinner 18:00-21:30. Call 01632 960124 to ask
about a table.

## Wi-Fi {#wifi}

The network is CampGuest. The password is on the card by the cabin door.

## Bins and recycling {#bins}

Bins are by the car park. Glass, cans and paper go in the green bins.

## Shop {#shop}

The camp shop is next to reception, open 08:00-18:00. It sells milk, bread and firewood.
